生计 LiveCalc · 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-27

1. 适用范围

「生计」（以下简称"本应用"）是一个记录商品价格、计算烹饪成本与营养分析的工具。

本应用采用自建服务器模式：你在首次使用时填写服务器地址，账号与业务数据保存在你或你所在组织配置的服务器上。本应用的开发者不运营该服务器，也不会从该服务器获取你的数据。若你使用他人提供的服务器，请同时查阅该服务提供者的隐私政策。

2. 本应用会处理哪些信息

你主动提供的信息：用户名、邮箱、手机号（可选）、密码（仅保存不可逆哈希）、昵称、头像、地区设置，以及你录入的价格记录、商家与坐标、菜谱与图片、试算方案、收藏、营养目标与预算、AI 使用偏好。

权限对应的信息（仅在你使用相应功能时）：

  • 相机：扫描商品条码。
  • 相册：选择头像或菜谱图片。
  • 位置：在地图上显示当前位置、记录商家坐标（仅使用期间，不后台定位）。
  • 本地存储：离线待同步队列与登录凭证（凭证加密保存在系统 Keychain / Keystore）。

本应用不收集通讯录、短信、通话记录、剪贴板内容或设备唯一标识，也不进行广告追踪。

3. 信息用途

提供并维护应用功能；计算成本与营养；生成菜谱推荐（规则或 AI）；数据同步与备份；保障安全与排查故障。

4. 第三方服务

  • 地图底图：高德、腾讯、OpenStreetMap 等瓦片服务会收到你的 IP 地址与地图请求参数。
  • AI 推荐：若服务器启用了 AI，菜品偏好等上下文会发送给该服务器配置的模型服务（可能是 OpenAI、Anthropic 或服务提供者自建的模型）。使用前应用会提示模型提供方与传输的数据类型。
  • 图片存储：服务器配置的本地存储或对象存储。

本应用不出售个人信息，也不与广告商共享数据。

5. 保存期限与删除

数据保存在你连接的服务器上，保存期限由该服务器的运营者决定。

应用内提供「数据导出」与「注销账号」。注销后将删除账号及其价格记录、商家、菜谱、方案、收藏、AI 设置与图片等数据；被其他用户引用的公共内容会匿名化保留。服务器备份可能由运营者按自身备份策略保留一段时间，可向其管理员咨询。

6. 你的权利

  • 查阅与更正：在个人中心编辑账号与设置。
  • 导出：使用「数据导出」获取你的数据副本。
  • 删除：使用「注销账号」删除账号与个人数据。
  • 撤回同意：在系统设置中撤销相机、相册、位置权限。
  • 投诉与咨询：通过下方联系方式。

7. 安全措施

建议为服务器启用 HTTPS；登录凭证加密保存在系统安全存储；密码经 SHA-256 与 bcrypt 处理，应用不保存明文密码。

8. 未成年人

本应用不面向 14 岁以下儿童单独提供服务。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用。

9. 政策变更

本政策更新时会在应用内展示并更新日期；重大变更会重新征求你的同意。

LiveCalc · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27

1. Scope

LiveCalc ("the App") records product prices, calculates cooking costs and analyses nutrition.

The App uses a self-hosted server model: on first launch you enter a server address, and your account and data are stored on the server you or your organisation operate. The App's developer does not operate that server and does not receive your data from it. If you use a server run by someone else, please also read that operator's privacy policy.

2. Information the App handles

Provided by you: username, email, optional phone number, password (stored only as an irreversible hash), nickname, avatar, region settings, and the price records, merchants and coordinates, recipes and images, meal plans, favourites, nutrition targets, budget and AI preferences you enter.

Information tied to device permissions (only when you use the feature):

  • Camera: scanning product barcodes.
  • Photo library: choosing an avatar or recipe image.
  • Location: showing your position on the map and recording merchant coordinates (while in use only, never in the background).
  • Local storage: an offline sync queue and login credentials (stored encrypted in the system Keychain / Keystore).

The App does not collect contacts, SMS, call logs, clipboard content or device identifiers, and does not run advertising tracking.

3. How information is used

To provide and maintain the App's features; to calculate costs and nutrition; to generate meal recommendations (rule-based or AI); to sync and back up your data; and to keep the service secure and diagnose faults.

4. Third-party services

  • Map tiles from AMap, Tencent Maps, OpenStreetMap and similar services receive your IP address and the map request parameters.
  • AI recommendations: if the server operator enables AI, meal preferences and similar context are sent to the model provider configured on that server (which may be OpenAI, Anthropic, or a self-hosted model). The App discloses the provider and the data types before first use.
  • Image storage: local storage or object storage configured on your server.

The App does not sell personal information and does not share it with advertisers.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is stored on the server you connect to; the retention period is decided by that server's operator.

The App provides data export and in-app account deletion. Deleting your account removes the account and its price records, merchants, recipes, meal plans, favourites, AI settings and images; public content referenced by other users is kept anonymised. Backups may be retained by the operator according to their own backup policy; ask the operator for details.

6. Your rights

  • Access and correction: edit your account and settings in the profile screen.
  • Portability: use data export to obtain a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: use account deletion to remove your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw consent: revoke camera, photo and location permissions in system settings.
  • Complaints: contact us using the address below.

7. Security

We recommend HTTPS for your server. Login credentials are stored in the system's secure storage, and passwords are processed with SHA-256 and bcrypt; the App never stores plaintext passwords.

8. Children

The App is not offered to children under 14 on their own. Minors should use it under the guidance of a guardian.

9. Changes

Updates to this policy are shown in the App with a new date; material changes require your consent again.
